What is RS WhatsApp?

RS WhatsApp is a WhatsApp bulk message sender and WhatsApp marketing software for Windows PC, made by Rameez Scripts. It connects a business number to the official WhatsApp Cloud API from Meta or links any WhatsApp number by QR code, then sends personalised bulk campaigns from Excel or CSV contact lists. It also answers incoming chats with an auto reply and an AI chatbot. With the official API, Meta bills the business directly and RS WhatsApp adds 0% markup on Meta's message fees.

How to send bulk WhatsApp messages from your PC

Six steps from install to your first campaign.

  1. Install RS WhatsAppDownload the Windows installer and start the 3-day free trial with Google.
  2. Link your numberPaste your Meta access token for the official WhatsApp Cloud API, or scan the QR code with your phone.
  3. Import your contactsPick a .xlsx or .csv file. Every column becomes a merge field such as {name} or {city}.
  4. Write the messageChoose an approved template on the official API, attach a photo, video or PDF, and turn on variations.
  5. Send now or scheduleWatch each message move from Sent to Delivered to Read.
  6. Let the AI answer repliesAuto reply and the AI chatbot handle the people who write back and save orders, bookings and leads.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best WhatsApp bulk sender for Windows PC?

RS WhatsApp is a Windows desktop WhatsApp bulk sender that sends through the official WhatsApp Cloud API from Meta or a QR-linked number, with scheduling, auto reply, an AI chatbot and Excel or CSV contacts in one app.

Does RS WhatsApp use the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. You can link a number to Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API with your own access token. A QR-code link is also available for numbers that are not on the API.

Do you add a markup on Meta's WhatsApp API fees?

No. Meta bills your own WhatsApp Business account directly at Meta's published rates. RS WhatsApp charges only its flat plan, with 0% markup on Meta's message fees.

Can my WhatsApp number get banned for bulk messages?

Any number can be restricted if people report it as spam. The official WhatsApp Cloud API with approved templates and opted-in contacts is the lowest-risk way to message customers in bulk. QR-linked numbers carry a higher risk.

Can I send bulk WhatsApp messages without saving numbers?

Yes. Import the numbers from an Excel or CSV file and send. You do not need to save each contact on your phone.

Can I schedule WhatsApp messages on PC?

Yes. Pick a date and time for any campaign and RS WhatsApp sends it then.

Does RS WhatsApp have an AI WhatsApp chatbot?

Yes. The built-in AI assistant answers customers with your own Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude or Grok key, learns from your website, understands photos and voice notes, and saves orders, bookings and leads.

Is RS WhatsApp a Chrome extension?

No. It is a Windows desktop app. It does not need Chrome or WhatsApp Web to stay open.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. A 3-day free trial with one number. Sign in with Google to start.

Is RS WhatsApp made by WhatsApp or Meta?

No. RS WhatsApp is an independent product by Rameez Scripts. It connects to Meta's public WhatsApp Cloud API but is not made, endorsed or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta.
